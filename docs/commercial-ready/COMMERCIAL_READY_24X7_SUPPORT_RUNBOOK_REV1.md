# StayQR 24×7 Support Runbook REV1

## Operating model

- Intake: 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
- Primary channel: the dedicated StayQR WhatsApp number after it is configured.
- Fallback channels: in-app support ticket and `support@stayqr.in`.
- After-hours owner: Founder.
- Support Guard: every hotel-access session remains explicit, reason-bound, audited and time-limited.

## Priority handling

- P0 / Critical: production outage, tenant-isolation/security incident, material data-loss or financial-integrity risk. Notify the founder immediately, preserve evidence, activate the incident runbook and use audited support access only when necessary.
- P1 / High: major hotel operation blocked with no reasonable normal workaround. Notify the founder after hours and begin triage.
- P2 / Medium: partial impairment or a working workaround. Record, classify and schedule follow-up without misusing emergency access.
- P3 / Low: general question, cosmetic defect or enhancement. Record for normal follow-up.

## Incident sequence

1. Record hotel, reporter, time, affected workflow and business impact.
2. Assign P0–P3 from evidence, not customer tone.
3. For P0/P1 after hours, notify the founder.
4. Check platform health and known provider incidents without entering hotel scope.
5. If hotel access is necessary, start a time-limited Support Guard session with the minimum permissions and a specific reason.
6. Record findings, workaround and next update in the ticket.
7. End Support Guard access immediately when the task is complete.
8. Close only after hotel confirmation or documented verification.

Acknowledgement/resolution times are not invented in this package. Any numeric contractual targets must be approved as a separate business decision and then configured in the support profile.
