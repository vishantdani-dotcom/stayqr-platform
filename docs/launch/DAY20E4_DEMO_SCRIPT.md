# StayQR v1.0 — Commercial Demo Script

**Target length:** 15–20 minutes  
**Audience:** independent/small hotel owner or decision maker

## Demo rules

- Use a demo/controlled hotel and synthetic guest data.
- Do not expose real guest KYC, payment credentials or production secrets.
- Demonstrate connected workflows rather than isolated screens.
- Do not claim deferred integrations/features listed in the v1.0 known-limitations document.

## 1. Opening — 60 seconds

Position StayQR as a cloud hotel operating system combining hotel operations with a signed QR guest experience.

Show:

- Dashboard
- property switcher/context
- occupancy/room/guest/request/order/revenue indicators

Message: the same tenant-scoped hotel data drives front desk, guest experience, finance and reporting.

## 2. Hotel setup — 90 seconds

Open **Hotel Setup** and show the five stages:

1. Hotel & Policies
2. Types, Floors & Rates
3. Rooms Import
4. Guest Operations
5. Readiness

Show that the readiness stage prevents an incomplete hotel from being presented as operational.

## 3. Reservation and inventory — 2 minutes

Open **Reservations** and **Booking Calendar**.

Demonstrate:

- reservation creation/record;
- room/date allocation;
- current room inventory/calendar visibility.

Explain that reservation and room state are connected rather than separate spreadsheets/pages.

## 4. Check-in and guest identity — 2 minutes

Use a controlled arrival/walk-in.

Show:

- guest search/reuse or new guest;
- stay dates;
- room selection;
- optional companion/identity fields;
- completed check-in.

Do not upload real KYC in a sales demo unless explicitly authorised and required.

## 5. Secure guest QR — 2 minutes

Open **QR Guides** for the active stay.

Show:

- signed guest guide QR/link;
- food-menu QR/link where enabled;
- Copy link / Download SVG;
- rotate/revoke controls.

Open the guest guide in a separate/incognito browser and show the hotel-branded guest experience.

Message: access is stay-bound and can expire/revoke/rotate; room number alone is not guest authentication.

## 6. Guest action — 2 minutes

Demonstrate at least one:

- food order; or
- service request.

Then switch to the hotel operations view and show the corresponding order/request appearing for staff processing.

## 7. Folio, payment and invoice — 3 minutes

Open **Folio & Settlement**.

Show:

- charges;
- collection/balance;
- controlled settlement action if appropriate to demo data.

Open **Invoices & Audit** and show invoice/receipt capability.

Message: financial state is tied to the guest/stay rather than maintained as disconnected notes.

## 8. Checkout and housekeeping — 2 minutes

Complete the controlled checkout.

Show:

- checkout validation;
- guest access invalidation behavior;
- housekeeping/room-turnover task/state.

This demonstrates the complete stay lifecycle from arrival through turnover.

## 9. Reports and operations — 2 minutes

Open **Reports** and show:

- occupancy/revenue KPIs;
- booking-source/payment-method or other drill-downs;
- CSV/PDF export capability.

Open **Operations Centre** and briefly show:

- support workspace;
- diagnostics/health;
- hotel system settings.

## 10. Commercial close — 60 seconds

Show **Legal & Policies** and explain that Privacy, Terms/AUP, DPA, SLA, Support, Subscription/Refund, Security and Cookie/Browser Storage documents are published.

State only the current published plan/room limits and commercial terms shown by StayQR at the time of the demo. For deployments beyond standard plan limits or multiple properties, discuss Enterprise / Custom rather than promising unsupported packaging.

## Demo acceptance checklist

A launch demo passes when the presenter can show this connected chain without a P0/P1 defect:

**hotel setup → reservation → check-in → guest QR → guest action → folio/payment → invoice → checkout → housekeeping → report/support**
