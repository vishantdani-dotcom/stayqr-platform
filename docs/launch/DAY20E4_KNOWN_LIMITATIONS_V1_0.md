# StayQR v1.0 — Launch Known Limitations

**Purpose:** prevent sales/support/product claims from exceeding the accepted v1.0 product and commercial scope.

These are launch-scope boundaries, not necessarily permanent product limitations.

## 1. Standard commercial scope

Current published standard plans are single-property plans with room limits up to the published Scale limit. Hotels requiring **more than 100 rooms, multiple properties or custom commercial terms** should use an Enterprise / Custom arrangement rather than being promised a standard-plan fit.

A commercial room limit is a plan/contract limit, not a claim about absolute database capacity.

## 2. OTA / channel manager

Certified OTA/channel-manager connectivity is not part of the v1.0 launch promise. Do not claim automatic Booking.com/MakeMyTrip/Expedia inventory/rate sync unless a separately verified integration is later implemented and accepted.

## 3. Native mobile applications

StayQR v1.0 is web-based. Native iOS/Android hotel-staff or guest apps are not part of the launch scope.

## 4. Smart locks and hardware

Direct smart-lock/door-lock integrations are not included in the launch scope.

## 5. Advanced enterprise modules

The following are not part of the standard v1.0 promise:

- advanced revenue-management automation;
- advanced loyalty program;
- enterprise-grade accounting/ERP integration parity;
- full enterprise PMS feature parity with products such as Oracle OPERA/large-chain systems.

## 6. Messaging automation

StayQR contains notification/support foundations and manual/adaptor-oriented messaging capabilities, but the launch scope must not be sold as a fully automated WhatsApp marketing/CRM platform.

No promotional/behavioural advertising capability should be inferred from the guest experience.

## 7. Payments

**Cashfree is the proven launch payment integration.** The schema contains historical/provider-neutral compatibility, but do not present Razorpay or another provider as an active v1.0 integration unless separately verified.

Payment-provider/bank/network availability remains outside StayQR's direct control.

## 8. Service level

The standard published availability commitment is a **99.5% monthly target**, subject to the published SLA exclusions.

Standard Starter/Growth/Scale plans do not include automatic service credits solely because the target is missed. Enhanced commitments require a separately signed Enterprise / Custom agreement.

## 9. Support coverage

Published staffed support baseline is **09:00–19:00 IST, Monday–Saturday**. Critical reports outside staffed hours are reasonable-efforts unless enhanced support has been separately agreed.

Do not promise 24×7 staffed support under the standard plan without changing the published policy and operational staffing.

## 10. Monitoring

StayQR has structured internal diagnostics and incident handling. The accepted Day 18 monitoring foundation does **not** claim that an external APM/monitoring vendor is configured.

## 11. Backup/recovery boundary

Database backup/restore and Supabase Storage object recovery are separate controls. A database-only restore does not prove that KYC/media object bytes have been restored.

Do not promise an RTO/RPO beyond an explicitly signed commercial commitment.

## 12. Hotel regulatory responsibility

StayQR provides software workflows, including identity/KYC and foreign-guest data fields, but the hotel remains responsible for its own property-specific statutory registrations, local records, tax treatment and lawful operational use.

Do not represent StayQR as automatically completing every hotel/government filing obligation unless the relevant submission integration is specifically implemented and verified.

## 13. Browser/device scope

StayQR is a responsive web application. Customer device, browser, hotel LAN/Wi-Fi and internet failures are outside StayQR's direct control and are treated according to the published SLA/support policies.

## 14. Final launch boundary

v1.0 is not final merely because these documents exist. Final launch remains gated by Day 20E completion, first controlled hotel go-live (20F) and final Go/No-Go (20G) with P0/P1 = 0 and accepted production evidence.
