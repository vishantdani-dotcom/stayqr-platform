# StayQR Day 20 — Operational Support Runbook

**Primary production/support owner:** Vishant Dani  
**Primary support channel:** support@stayqr.in

This runbook operationalises the published StayQR Support + Escalation Policy. It does not replace the public policy.

## 1. Standard support window

Published staffed support baseline:

**09:00–19:00 IST, Monday–Saturday**

Requests may be submitted at any time. Critical reports outside staffed hours are handled on a reasonable-efforts basis unless a separately signed agreement provides enhanced coverage.

## 2. Minimum ticket intake

Capture:

- hotel/property;
- requester/account identity;
- affected feature;
- issue start time;
- severity/business impact;
- affected room/reservation/guest session/transaction reference where relevant;
- screenshot or sanitized error text;
- incident/request ID if generated by StayQR Diagnostics;
- workaround available: yes/no;
- whether security, privacy, tenant isolation, payment or financial integrity may be involved.

Never request passwords, JWTs, service-role/API secrets, full card details, raw payment payloads or unnecessary KYC documents.

## 3. Severity and acknowledgement targets

### P0 — Critical

Examples: widespread core outage, confirmed tenant isolation failure, material security incident, material data-loss/financial-integrity risk.

**Acknowledgement target:** within 2 staffed-support hours.

### P1 — High

Major production functionality materially unavailable or a critical hotel workflow blocked without a reasonable normal workaround.

**Acknowledgement target:** within 4 staffed-support hours.

### P2 — Medium

Partial impairment, isolated defect or practical workaround exists.

**Acknowledgement target:** within 1 business day.

### P3 — Low

Question, cosmetic issue, documentation request, feature request or low-impact problem.

**Acknowledgement target:** within 2 business days.

Acknowledgement is not a guaranteed resolution time.

## 4. Triage flow

1. Confirm hotel/property and environment.
2. Reproduce safely if possible; destructive testing belongs in staging/disposable environments.
3. Open **Operations Centre → Diagnostics**.
4. Check health, unresolved incidents, failed deliveries and invalid-index/query health.
5. Search by incident ID, request ID, error code or sanitized message.
6. Classify P0/P1/P2/P3.
7. Acknowledge the diagnostic incident when ownership is accepted.
8. Record workaround/next action in the support thread.
9. Escalate to incident handling for P0/P1, security, tenant isolation, financial integrity or suspected data loss.
10. Resolve the diagnostic incident only after the underlying issue is fixed and verified; reopen if it recurs.

## 5. Common routing

### Login / staff access

- confirm the correct identity and hotel;
- confirm invite/role/status;
- do not share passwords or bypass role controls;
- owner/manager may manage staff where permitted.

### Reservation / room allocation

- preserve reservation ID and room/date details;
- do not force overlapping inventory through SQL;
- escalate reproducible availability/double-booking defects immediately.

### Guest QR

- confirm active stay and guide publication state;
- validate expiry/revocation/rotation behavior;
- never request the raw signed token in ordinary support notes if an incident/reference ID is sufficient.

### Payments / folio / invoice

- preserve provider/order/payment/folio/invoice references;
- do not duplicate collection/refund actions while status is uncertain;
- reconcile provider and StayQR state before corrective action;
- escalate Cashfree provider issues to Cashfree when required.

### KYC/privacy

- use authorized product controls;
- avoid copying raw documents into tickets;
- privacy/grievance matters follow the Privacy Policy/DPA channels and handling requirements.

## 6. Support workspace

Where product support workspace functionality is used:

- keep ticket status/priority/owner accurate;
- preserve ticket history;
- use audited safe-support access rather than shared credentials where applicable;
- end support access when the support task is finished.

## 7. Evidence and privacy boundary

Use the existing monitoring rule:

Never log raw guest tokens, invoice-verification tokens, JWTs, passwords, cookies, authorization headers, email addresses, phone numbers, payment payloads, KYC documents or arbitrary application state.

Use incident/request IDs and sanitized context instead.

## 8. Closure

A support issue may be closed when:

- the fix/workaround has been communicated;
- the affected workflow is revalidated;
- related diagnostic incidents are correctly resolved/linked;
- any financial/security/privacy follow-up is assigned;
- the customer/hotel does not have an unresolved P0/P1 impact from the same issue.
