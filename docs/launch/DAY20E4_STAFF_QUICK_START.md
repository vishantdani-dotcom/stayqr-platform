# StayQR v1.0 — Staff Quick Start

**For hotel staff using StayQR**

## 1. Sign in safely

- Use the invitation sent to your own email/identity.
- Do not share passwords or owner accounts.
- Confirm the hotel/property shown in StayQR before performing an action.
- If you cannot access a section, ask your hotel owner/manager to review your role/permissions; do not borrow another user's login.

## 2. Common role views

Actual access can be permission-driven, but the launch role baseline is:

### Reception / Front Desk

Typical areas:

- Dashboard
- Reservations
- Booking Calendar
- Rooms
- Guests
- Check-In/Out
- Payments / Folio
- Service Requests
- Invoices

Core rule: verify guest, room, stay dates and financial state before confirming a front-desk action.

### Housekeeping

Typical areas:

- Dashboard
- Rooms
- Housekeeping
- Maintenance
- Service Requests

Core rule: use the current room/task status and update it only after the physical work is performed.

### Restaurant

Typical areas:

- Dashboard
- Menu Management
- Food Orders

Core rule: keep item availability accurate and progress orders through the real kitchen/service lifecycle.

### Accounts

Typical areas:

- Dashboard
- Payments
- Folio & Settlement
- Charges
- Reports
- Invoices & Audit
- Operations Centre where permitted

Core rule: never use an unsupported manual database change to force balances, invoices or payment status.

### Owner / Manager

Broader hotel administration is available, including hotel setup, staff, guest guide, reporting and system settings according to permissions.

## 3. Guest-data rules

- Access only the guest information needed for your work.
- Do not export/copy guest data for personal use.
- Do not email or message KYC documents unless the hotel's lawful process specifically requires it and the channel is authorised.
- Do not share signed guest QR links outside the intended guest/stay.
- Revoke/rotate guest access through StayQR when required; do not edit signed tokens manually.

## 4. If something fails

1. Do not repeat a financial/destructive action rapidly.
2. Capture the error message, time and affected room/reservation/transaction reference.
3. Tell the owner/manager or authorised support contact.
4. For a serious outage, suspected data leak, financial-integrity issue or security incident, stop the affected workflow until it is assessed.

Support: **support@stayqr.in**

Never send a password, API/service-role secret or full card credentials to support.
